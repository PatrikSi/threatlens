# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ThreatLens contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to keep collaboration respectful, constructive, and professional.

Examples of expected behavior:

- assuming good intent while still giving direct technical feedback
- making room for questions from contributors with different backgrounds
- crediting others for ideas, research, and implementation work
- focusing critique on the work, not the person

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or exclusionary language
- deliberately hostile review behavior or repeated bad-faith arguments
- publishing private or security-sensitive information without permission

## Reporting

For project moderation concerns that can be shared publicly, open a minimal issue at `https://github.com/PatrikSi/threatlens/issues/new` without posting sensitive personal details.
For private, platform-level, or maintainer-conflict conduct concerns, use GitHub's abuse reporting path at `https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse`.
Do not post personal contact details, private messages, or security-sensitive information in public issues.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and keep the project workable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, and maintainer-run community channels related to ThreatLens.
